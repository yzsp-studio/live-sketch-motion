## 🇨🇳 中文

实景手绘动效技能，从实景照片、参考视频或文字场景生成一段短的实景手绘显影短片。它的核心不是普通白色描边，而是三层同时成立：真实场景在手绘开始前就先自然动起来；前景出现一只手拿亮蓝色马克笔；手绘层从笔尖位置逐帧生长，笔尖没经过的位置绝不提前显影。最大难点是主体在手绘前、中、后都要保持活体运动——不能停下来等笔画，手绘层必须继承主体原有的运动路径与节奏。流程为理解输入、产出制作方案文档、产出详细视频提示词、生成视频、质量检查；文字场景会先生成真实照片感预览图等用户确认。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 实景照片 / 参考视频 / 文字场景 → 实景手绘显影短片
- 笔尖驱动显影：笔没经过的位置不会提前出现，也不整块弹出
- 主体全程不冻结，手绘层继承原有运动轨迹、眨眼、呼吸与摆尾
- 三层手绘结构：厚实硬边白边 + 黑色简化轮廓 + 彩铅斜向排线填充
- 文字场景先出真实照片感预览图，确认构图与接触关系后再动效
- 显影从主体左上向右下推进、画完手自然撤出，背景只做可信微动，默认 5 秒单镜、音频字幕默认关闭

## 🇬🇧 English

A live-sketch motion skill that turns a real-scene photo, reference video, or text scene into a short hand-drawn reveal clip. The effect is not generic white outlining but three conditions holding at once: the real scene must move naturally before drawing starts, a hand holding a bright blue marker enters in the foreground, and the sketch layer grows frame by frame from the pen tip so nothing appears where the pen has not passed. The hard part is keeping the subject alive before, during, and after conversion — it never freezes to wait for strokes, and the sketch layer inherits the subject's original motion path and rhythm. The flow runs input understanding, a production plan doc, a detailed video prompt doc, generation, and a QC pass; text-only scenes first get a photorealistic preview image for confirmation.

### ✨ What it does

- Photo, reference video, or text scene to a live hand-drawn reveal clip
- Pen-tip-driven reveal: nothing shows before the tip passes, no block pop-in
- Subject never freezes; the sketch inherits its motion path, blinks, breathing, tail flicks
- Three-layer sketch structure: thick hard white edge, black simplified outline, colored-pencil hatching
- Text-only scenes get a photorealistic preview first so framing and ground contact are confirmed
- Reveal sweeps top-left to bottom-right, the hand exits naturally, background stays subtly alive; default 5s single shot with audio and subtitles off
